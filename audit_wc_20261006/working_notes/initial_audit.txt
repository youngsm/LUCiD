SK WAND data mode physics audit
6 October 2026

UPDATED AFTER THE EXPANDED PHOTONSIM AUDIT

The expanded audit found major source-physics errors that supersede the first
pass's assessment of priorities. PhotonSim assigns far too many stopped pi-
and mu- to hydrogen in water. Correcting pion target selection reduces emitted
Cherenkov light by 25.0% in a 200 MeV pi- sample; the baseline produces spurious
pi0 flashes. Negative-muon capture is 12.86% versus the measured 18.4%, distorting
the Michel population and time distribution. EM nuclear interactions and
radiative pion-capture channels are also missing. GENIE is explicitly in the
user's production scope alongside the supplied particle configurations.

Read photonsim_deep/REPORT.txt for the expanded, prioritized findings, executed
minimal reproductions, candidate fixes, physical references and coverage.
Its lane reports preserve the underlying source, measurements and limitations.
Quoted PhotonSim photon changes are emitted light, not measured detector PE.
The target-selection prototypes do not fix every capture final-state model.

The first-pass findings below remain valid: missing decay-spin correlations,
missing high-charge PMT compression, a small PMT-intersection precision defect
and fine-grained photon truth-label errors. Absolute PMT efficiency remains a
potentially large unresolved calibration question: the supplied QE table has
no adequate provenance establishing whether collection efficiency is included.

This conclusion applies to LUCiD 20df3094bd563162d40c6ca34c5ad25e1e1d3656,
config/SK_WAND_geom_config.json and config/SK_WAND_physics_config.json, with
the actual production settings. PhotonSim was independently built from the

11.3.0. The audit did not change production code or configurations.

Every numerical test and source build used milano CPU allocations. No GPUs

1. Missing pion to muon to Michel spin correlation

Priority: material angular-physics defect for affected decay chains.
Location: PhotonSim src/PhysicsList.cc, instantiated by production, plus the
LUCiD creator-process categorization required when applying the fix.

PhotonSim registers G4DecayPhysics without the pion/muon spin-aware decay
processes. That erases the joint angular correlation between a pion-decay
muon and its Michel positron. This is distinct from the accepted approximation
of unpolarized optical scattering.

Minimal physical experiment: generate 2,000 stopped pi+ chains in water, retain
the existing late cut, and compare the muon's birth direction with positrons
above 40 MeV kinetic energy. Cherenkov generation is disabled only for this decay
experiment; charged-particle propagation and decay run in the real source.

  Current source:             forward hemisphere fraction = 0.529670
  Independent water oracle:   forward hemisphere fraction = 0.383488
  Isolated corrected source:  forward hemisphere fraction = 0.384522

The oracle integrates the leading-order Michel distribution using measured
positive-muon polarization retention in water adopted by SK. The current result fails the physical
test by about nine reference standard errors. The corrected result agrees.
The precise size is for this controlled selection, not all WAND events or PE.

Production applicability was checked separately: 20 actual 1 GeV pi+ events
contained three pion->muon->Michel chains with Cherenkov-emitting muons, whose
birth kinetic energies were 96.7, 1020.5 and 701.7 MeV. Their direct muon light
counts were 2,569, 191,216 and 115,585 photons. Thus a visible correlated chain
is reachable. This small sample does not establish its campaign frequency.
The stopped-pion test's 4.12 MeV muons themselves are below threshold, so the
standalone Michel marginal direction remains isotropic; the defect is the
joint kinematics, not a demonstrated total-light deficit.

Minimal reproducer, after generating the preserved fixtures:

  python audit_wc_20261006/photonsim_physics/test_spin_physics.py baseline
  # AssertionError: Missing physical pion/muon spin correlation
  python audit_wc_20261006/photonsim_physics/test_spin_physics.py water
  # passes against the same physical oracle

The small test evaluates:

  expected = 0.5 - water_retention * integrated_Michel_asymmetry / 4
  sigma = sqrt(expected * (1 - expected) / selected_positron_count)
  assert abs(measured_fraction - expected) < 4 * sigma + 0.01

Minimal adequate fix proposal:

  #include "G4SpinDecayPhysics.hh"
  RegisterPhysics(new G4DecayPhysics(0));
  RegisterPhysics(new G4SpinDecayPhysics(0));

Also model calibrated stopping-water depolarization. The tested illustrative
mu+ correction retains the full spin with probability 0.718 at stopping and
randomizes the remaining fraction. Merely multiplying the polarization vector
by 0.718 is incorrect for this Geant4 channel, which uses it as a rotation
axis rather than an asymmetry magnitude. Finally, add "DecayWithSpin" to
lucid/sources/particle_categorization.py's DECAY_PROCESSES, since that is the
corrected muon daughter's creator-process name. Otherwise physical photons
survive but the Michel category can disappear.

The patch summary is photonsim_physics/minimal_spin_fix.diff. The PhotonSim
variants PhysicsList_spin.cc and SteppingAction_spin_water.cc were executed;
the LUCiD creator-process integration change remains proposed. Radiative and
finite-mass corrections make the leading-order oracle approximate; their
scale, and the retention uncertainty, do not explain the baseline discrepancy.
The fixture generators are run_audit.sbatch and run_spin_water.sbatch; the
source, macros and analysis are retained in that directory. Negative-muon
capture is now tested in photonsim_deep/transport; polarized GENIE input and
remaining bound-muon physics are discussed in the expanded audit.

Primary physical reference:
https://arxiv.org/html/2403.08619v2, Sections III.2 and III.3.
Geant4's spin process implementation is recorded in photonsim_physics/reference/.
Full evidence: photonsim_physics/findings.txt, spin_results.json,
production_chain_results.json and final-checks-39969890.log.

2. Reflected photons can numerically encounter the same PMT again

Priority: secondary numerical defect; small measured total-light effect.
Location: lucid/propagation/base.py:229, with photon_step.py's 0.1 mm nudge.

The sphere discriminant b*b - 4*a*c subtracts large, almost equal float32
quantities at SK distances. The resulting entry point can be roughly 1 mm
inside the modeled PMT. The 0.1 mm reflection nudge then leaves the ray inside.
When the next candidate map includes this PMT, its exit is treated as another
surface encounter. Its deposit is zero, but another reflection/absorption
decision can change or terminate the reflected ray.

This is a precision defect within the accepted spherical PMT representation.
It is not the accepted incompleteness of the endpoint PMT search.

On JAX 0.11.0 CPU, in a fused, actual sampled-step test, 1,820 of 44,806 reflected PMT rays falsely
re-encountered their original sensor (4.06%). The isolated stable formula gave
zero in 44,822 reflected rays. These are targeted geometry probes, not the
fraction of all photons lost.

A separate JAX/jaxlib 0.4.38 CPU runtime, matching the pinned JAX version,
also reproduces it: 1,780 false encounters in 44,896 reflections (3.96%);
the isolated fix gives zero in 44,906. This is a pip environment with Python
3.12, not an execution of the production container. The old one-ray trigger
does not reproduce on this version because compiler rounding differs; use
the version-specific reproducer or the preserved population test.

The total-response check on JAX 0.11.0 used 6 million paired K=12 DATA photon
draws with the real SK_WAND optical settings. Corrected minus current charge
was -295 +/- 72.3 PE out of 396,553.6 PE, or -0.0744% +/- 0.0182%. No significant
late-light change was resolved. This is a real but small effect in the tested
isotropic, uniform-volume, 300--650 nm ensemble. It is not a measured Geant4
event-cohort bias. It measures the entire discriminant replacement, including
changes to first-encounter classification and normals; it does not isolate
the loss caused by repeated encounters. The earlier smaller sample's late-tail
hint did not persist.

Minimal executable reproducer using the preserved JAX 0.4.38 interpreter
(inside an allowed allocation, with the environment from Section 8):

  audit_wc_20261006/runtime_jax0438/venv/bin/python \
    audit_wc_20261006/geometry/repro_reflection_selfhit_pinned.py
  audit_wc_20261006/runtime_jax0438/venv/bin/python \
    audit_wc_20261006/geometry/repro_reflection_selfhit_pinned.py --fixed

The current calculation falsely encounters PMT 1027 again after 0.285 mm.
The corrected calculation reaches the next real surface 0.703 m away. Both
standalone checks completed in milano job 39970270. The separate older
repro_reflection_selfhit.py demonstrates a different ray under JAX 0.11.0:
a seed-0 false encounter after 87 micrometres, removed by the correction for
all 32 checked seeds. These scripts change the function only in memory.

Minimal fix, replacing the cancellation-prone discriminant:

  perp = oc - (jnp.sum(oc * ray_d, axis=1) / a)[:, None] * ray_d
  discriminant = 4 * a * (sensor_radius**2 - jnp.sum(perp**2, axis=1))

Keep the existing root selection. Increasing the global nudge merely hides
the inaccurate intersection and introduces a larger artificial path change.
Evidence: geometry/findings.txt, repro_reflection_selfhit_pinned.py,
fused_results_jax0.11.0.json and charge_bias_summary.json. Pinned-version
evidence is runtime_jax0438/fused_results_jax0.4.38.json.

3. Some photons have the wrong creation-segment truth label

Priority: small truth-label error; total light and particle ownership survive.
Location: PhotonSim src/DataManager.cc:464--473.

PhotonSim infers a photon's source segment from its emission timestamp and
the parent's segment start times. That inference sometimes chooses the next
step. In ten 2.2 MeV gamma events, 32 of 1,817 photon positions unambiguously
lie on a different same-parent segment. In the clearest example the photon
is 25.3 micrometres from its assigned segment but 6 nanometres from the correct
one. Other photons near boundaries cannot be resolved by float32 positions.

Minimal invariant and actual executed reproducer:

  counts = np.bincount(photon_segment_index, minlength=len(segment_ncherenkov))
  assert np.array_equal(counts, segment_ncherenkov)

  python audit_wc_20261006/photonsim_physics/test_segment_counts.py
  # First gamma event: 144 photons, absolute segment-count mismatch 8; fails.

The independent position test is segment_ownership.py. Mismatched counts also
occur in the electron, muon and pion fixtures. They do not imply those photons
were lost; the raw/event/segment total photon counts agree.

Minimal reliable fix: attach the parent's local step index to each optical
secondary when it is created, then translate that index through the existing
per-parent segment offset. Do not reconstruct creation provenance from time
or nearest position. The expanded audit now validates an isolated implementation:
1,206 wrong assignments among 766,427 photons become zero, with all photon
position/direction/wavelength/time arrays bitwise unchanged. Each wrong original
assignment uses a Geant4 birth time slightly beyond the actual emitting step's
end; first-step timestamp subtraction is not the cause. Production is unchanged.
Evidence: photonsim_deep/emission/step_tag_prototype.diff, step_tag_results.json
and findings.md; earlier photonsim_physics/segment_ownership_results.json.

4. Potentially large unresolved absolute PMT efficiency

SK_WAND uses SK_QE.json as an incident-photon detection probability, with
qe_corrections=1. At 400 nm this is 0.2261907458. There is no independent
photoelectron collection-efficiency factor. The exact curve's measurement
source, illumination conditions and collection-efficiency convention could
not be established from the file or its introduction in git.

This matters because published R3600 specifications distinguish photocathode
QE from collection efficiency, while SK calibration literature sometimes uses
"QE" for their product. The curve's roughly 23% peak resembles photocathode
specifications, but resemblance does not prove its convention.

Conditional diagnostic, not a confirmed defect or measured event bias:

  from lucid.wavelength.medium import load_qe_curve
  q = float(load_qe_curve("config/pmt/SK_QE.json")(400.))
  print(q, q * .73, 1 / .73 - 1)
  # 0.2261907458, 0.1651192445, 0.3698630137

If this is bare cathode QE, collection efficiency .73 or .67 implies the
present direct-PE normalization is respectively 37% or 49% too high. If it
already includes collection, another factor would recreate a double-counting
bug. Some collection values depend on aperture; the cited Nishimura paper
also contains a 67% table versus 73% prose discrepancy at the same aperture.

Resolution: recover the source table and convention, or compare absolute
low-occupancy response with a calibrated photon flux. If it is cathode QE,
use an appropriate measured collection factor through qe_corrections or a
measured full detection-efficiency curve. No arbitrary CE factor was applied.

Primary references, calculation, and provenance analysis:
physics_crosscheck/findings.md and optical_reference_results.json.
https://inspirehep.net/files/f043ea4dfbaf066f1e0ec243e6d87f15
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.0162
https://s3.cern.ch/inspire-prod-files-f/fd23eeff10e09b37f7ce3283940a852f

5. Additional physical and secondary-path findings

High-charge PMT nonlinearity is absent from the readout. The actual SK_WAND
charge function remains additive: 300,000 samples at each of 30, 200, 1,190
and 5,000 input PE give relative gain at 1,190 PE of 1.000093, normalized to
30 PE. This is distinct from the accepted shape of the one-PE distribution.
SK calibration Section 3.1.7 and Figure 19 measure high-charge compression
of the ID PMTs; the text reports deviations exceeding 10% above 1,000 PE.
It identifies PMT anode response separately from the QBEE electronics. This
does not establish a particular correction at exactly 1,190 PE, nor transfer
a short-pulse calibration unchanged to every 200 ns integrated digit.

The ordinary ten-event production bomb cohort contains one >=1,000-PE digit
among 58,690 saved digits. Its 1,190 PE account for 3.13% of that event's
truth charge and 0.413% of the cohort's truth charge. This establishes that
high integrated occupancy is reachable; these fractions are not missing-
charge estimates or precise campaign frequencies. Replaying this event with
the identical keys reproduces its detected charge exactly and resolves the
pulse-width question: 1,152 PE arrive within a 1 ns sliding window, and the
central 90% span is 0.766 ns. The 132 ns full span is a small late tail. Thus
the production witness has a genuinely prompt high-occupancy optical pulse.
These are pre-PMT-TTS arrival times; the exact anode response still needs a
matched calibration. See end_to_end/bright_digit_timing.json, job 39970809.

Minimal current-code response probe (inside an allowed CPU allocation):

  import numpy as np
  from lucid.simulation.digitizer import apply_readout_resolution, resolve_model_config
  import json
  cfg = json.load(open("config/SK_WAND_physics_config.json"))
  model = resolve_model_config(cfg["digitizer"])
  rng = np.random.default_rng(1901190)
  for n in (30, 1190):
      q, _ = apply_readout_resolution(np.full(300_000, n),
                                     np.zeros(300_000), model, rng)
      print(n, q.mean() / n)  # gain stays approximately one

The executed probe includes a full 1,190 simultaneous-photon digitizer call:
digitizer/probe_high_charge_response.py, high_charge_results.json, milano
job 39970669. The minimal physically justified fix is a measured, period-
appropriate PMT charge-response function in apply_readout_resolution, before
charge-dependent timing. Preserve true PE in truth records. An arbitrary
clamp or universal 10% reduction would not be a calibrated fix. Pulse-width
dependence needs measurement or a supported response model.
References/evidence: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1307.0162, Section 3.1.7;
digitizer/high_charge_finding.txt and end_to_end/bomb_occupancy.json.

Water asymmetric scattering imports a coefficient fitted with SK's forward
law p(mu)=2*mu on 0<=mu<=1, but uses Henyey--Greenstein with g=.95. The mean
cosine becomes .95 instead of 2/3. Its rate is small: a straight-path estimate
weighted by Cherenkov spectrum, QE and absorption gives roughly 0.30% affected
over 20 m. This is not a demonstrated large charge error. Minimal reproducer:

  import jax.numpy as jnp
  from lucid.wavelength.scattering import hg_sample_cos_theta
  u = (jnp.arange(1_000_000) + .5) / 1_000_000
  print(hg_sample_cos_theta(u, .95).mean(), jnp.sqrt(u).mean())
  # .95 versus .6666666

Use mu=sqrt(U), uniform azimuth, for that specific SK fitted phase law; or
refit HG and its coefficient together. Changing HG g alone does not recover
the law. See physics_crosscheck/verify_optical_reference.py and findings.md.

The scat10x diagnostic has a substantial truncation issue, separate from
canonical SK_WAND. Its intended fiTQun AttenL job path still defaults to K=12.
Relative to K=96 it misses 31.8% of detected light at 300 nm, 11.5% at 325 nm,
and 2.63% at 350 nm in the probe. K=96 had no detected tail in its final 24
iterations for that sample. Expose the existing --K option through the job
configuration and check convergence when changing scattering strength.
Reproducer: simulator_statistics/check_statistics.py with
AUDIT_PHYSICS_CONFIG=config/SK_WAND_physics_config_scat10x.json, AUDIT_K=96,
and AUDIT_RESULT_NAME=scat10x_K96_results.json. Set a distinct result name
to preserve the canonical results.json evidence.
Canonical SK_WAND showed no extra light between K=12 and K=24 in its test.

Pileup merging drops segment group_id, so two streams containing [0,0] each
write [0,1,2,3] instead of [0,0,1,1]. Preserve group IDs and offset groups
between streams. This changes grouping truth, not sensor charge or timing,
and is outside the supplied single-vertex SK_WAND GeV production path.
Minimal reproducer: production_integrity/repro_pileup_group_ids.py.

6. Independent checks supporting the conclusions

The checks below test specific invariants or physical laws; their success is
not a claim that every possible input or every real-detector effect is right.

Source physics: 40 e-/mu-/pi+/gamma events conserve total emitted photons;
primary energies and emission band agree with input; 40 pion replacement
endpoints are continuous. A high-beta muon Frank--Tamm comparison gives
observed/expected yield 0.998648. A 20,000-event GENIE injection test gives
mean cos(theta)=-0.00340 and mean cos(theta)^2=.333384.

Input handoff: reversed/noncontiguous ROOT chunks preserve ordering, mm->m
conversion, wavelength, direction and emission time. Synthetic later-step
and candidate records survive chunking; padding does not. 513 known photons
reach 513 intended actual PMTs with exact IDs and <.0063 ns timing residual.
513 outside-detector sources give zero ID hits. Orphan truth labels do not
remove physical PE. See root_emission/findings.md.

Absolute transport law: 2.4 million photons over 24 wavelength, distance and
reflectance settings agree with independently evaluated absorption/scattering
survival times incident QE. Combined chi-square=28.944 for 24 degrees of
freedom, p=.222, maximum deviation 2.739 sigma. At 400 nm over 20 m the expected
19,162.8 PE compare with 19,093 at R=0 and 19,125 at R=.25. The recent double-QE
and reflection-normalization fixes behave correctly against this oracle.
This validates the use of the supplied QE, not its experimental calibration.

Transport/statistics: 27 independent stochastic and angular checks pass;
water coefficient equations and all 25 red Pope--Fry knots agree with cited
values. Nominal K24 has no detected contribution beyond step 12 in 65,536
photons. Another 65,536-photon DATA sample has 13,017 valid detected records,
with no photon contributing multiple valid hits. Tiny clipped residual
weights have infinite timestamps and are discarded before digitization.

Geometry: 200,000 cylinder rays agree with independent double-precision
intersections: zero missing/wrong faces, worst distance error 48 micrometres.
11,096 PMTs are loaded without overlapping spheres. See geometry/findings.txt.

Readout: 400 independent integration, trigger and decomposition cases pass,
plus one million PE timing samples. Tests include cross-window behavior,
negative absolute timestamps, threshold application, dark labeling, and
charge-dependent timing. Production settings and sparse-file preservation,
ancestry, containment, seed separation and volume sampling were also checked.
See digitizer/findings.txt and production_integrity/REPORT.txt.

Actual ROOT-to-HDF5 production: five 100 MeV electrons, five 1 GeV muons and
ten 1 GeV pions all passed with the normal SK_WAND trigger. Nine of ten
2.2 MeV gamma events survived a separately labeled diagnostic selection of
at least three physical hits; that gamma selection is not the GeV trigger.
Persisted particle and segment physics-charge sums agree within 6.14e-6 PE,
all retained source-to-PMT timing lower bounds pass, and file references are
consistent. No detected PE lacked particle/segment attribution in this sample.
Its largest per-PMT truth charge was 174 PE. Absolute experimental SK energy
calibration is not established by these consistency checks.
Evidence: end_to_end/results.json and slurm-39969996.log.

An additional ten events from the ordinary GeV/01_pbomb.json production
configuration generated 5,413,607 photons. All ten survived the normal
trigger. Every raw primary ID, PDG and kinetic energy survived into metadata;
stored ancestors matched the raw Geant4 parent chains. There were zero orphan
PE, maximum per-(particle,digit) charge disagreement was 1.34e-5 PE, and the
smallest source-to-PMT causal slack was positive at 0.000145 ns. This cohort
reached 1,190 truth PE in one digit, with 1,154 reconstructed PE, so high-charge
PMT response is relevant to the supplied production configuration. The
reconstructed value fluctuates from SPE sampling and is not evidence that
nonlinearity is modeled. Evidence: end_to_end/results_bomb.json,
bomb.sbatch and bomb-39970274.log; milano job 39970274 completed successfully.

7. Remaining calibration and validation limits

Absolute PMT efficiency is the most consequential unresolved normalization
question. Scalar reflection parameters and angular response require optical
calibration; config comments do not establish their accuracy. Spatial and
time-dependent water properties are simplified. PMT response has no high-
occupancy nonlinearity; the ordinary production sample reaches high charge,
but its calibrated pulse-dependent correction remains undetermined.
These omissions require detector measurements and broader occupancy checks,
not an arbitrary parameter patch.

Full nuclear-model accuracy, all energy/species combinations, material outside
the full water tank, negative-muon spin/bound-decay response, and every possible
geometry trajectory were not established by these finite probes. Negative-muon
capture rates were subsequently checked and a large discrepancy established;
see the expanded report. The user's
explicitly accepted approximations are listed in SCOPE.txt and were not
reclassified as bugs.

8. Reproduction and handoff

All reported numerical results were obtained in milano Slurm allocations.
Unsuccessful setup attempts are also preserved and are not passing evidence.
To run an individual Python test, first obtain an allowed allocation:

  srun --partition=milano --account=neutrino:ml-dev@milano --qos=preemptable \
       --ntasks=1 --cpus-per-task=4 --mem=16G --time=00:30:00 \
       --pty bash --noprofile --norc

Then from the repository root set JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu, PYTHONPATH=$PWD, and
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1. Use the interpreter documented by the relevant
lane, with PYTHONNOUSERSITE=1 for the isolated pinned runtime. The shared
initial Python environment provides JAX 0.11.0; the separate
runtime_jax0438 directory records the pinned-version check. Never run these
tests on the login node. A roma allocation with the matching account is also
within the user's allowed resources.

The 27 optical-law checks pass on both JAX versions. The geometry population
defect and its isolated correction were also verified on both. The six-million-
photon charge comparison and the main integration runs use JAX 0.11.0; their
precise small numerical effects are not asserted to be identical on every
backend. An attempted shared SIF launch failed during mounting before Python
started; its logs remain under optical_transport/pinned_container/ and are
not counted as successful validation.

Full PhotonSim fixture regeneration additionally uses the existing CVMFS
Geant4/ROOT installation and the prior audit's Apptainer rootfs/runtime
wrapper. Their exact paths are recorded in photonsim_physics/run_audit.sbatch
and geant4_env.sh. The source/macros and numerical analysis are preserved here,
but this directory alone is not a portable Geant4 installation.

The small physical regression tests deliberately fail for the confirmed
defects; diagnostic measurement scripts may instead assert that the erroneous
behavior is present and print it. The geometry --fixed option and spin source
variants are isolated demonstrations, not applied production fixes.
