Production route is Cylinder.intersect_ray -> propagation/cylinder.py:
batch_intersect_cylinder_with_grid, not propagation/geometry.py. A source
comment incorrectly says the former is not the active path. Surface normals
are chosen from the intersection part flags, so the tiny cap-normal tolerance
in the unused unified helper is not a production finding.

Confirmed bug: float32 PMT entry calculation can cause a reflected photon
to immediately intersect the same PMT again.

Mechanism
  propagation/base.py computes the sphere discriminant as b*b - 4*a*c.
  At SK distances the O(1000 m^2) terms cancel down to O(0.1 m^2), losing
  enough precision to put the returned PMT entry point about 1 mm inside its
  modeled surface. photon_step.py nudges only 0.1 mm outward. If the originating
  PMT is a candidate on the next leg, base.py accepts its forward exit as a
  fresh sensor encounter. The _ahead gate suppresses charge for that old PMT,
  but does not suppress its geometry flag. The subsequent photon step applies
  another sensor reflect/absorb decision; reflected light can be lost or its
  direction changed. This is a numerical issue within the accepted sphere
  approximation, not a claim that spherical PMTs themselves are a bug.

Minimal suggested fix (not applied to production)
  Replace the cancellation-prone discriminant with a perpendicular-offset
  calculation using the same normalized ray and projection:

    perp = oc - (jnp.sum(oc * ray_d, axis=1) / a)[:, None] * ray_d
    discriminant = 4 * a * (sensor_radius**2 - jnp.sum(perp**2, axis=1))

  This retains the existing stable root selection while making the small
  half-chord quantity local to the PMT. A complete closest-point/half-chord
  root formulation is another option. Do not just enlarge the global nudge:
  that masks the entry error and introduces a larger artificial optical path.

Other independently checked invariants
  200,000 interior isotropic cylinder rays compared to independent float64
  analytic roots: zero missed intersections, zero wrong wall/cap classifications,
  max distance error 47.93 um; 99.9th percentile error 7.84 um.
  SK_WAND loads 11,096 PMTs: 7,752 barrel, 1,672 on each cap.
  R=16.962280245 m, H=36.378422222 m, PMT radius=0.254 m.
  Minimum PMT center separation 0.697731664 m (> two PMT radii).
  Largest measured-position projection to cylinder is 28.725 mm.
  Actual production uses K=12, temperature=0 (hard overlap), and the leg-bound
  switch is True. First-hit weighting and first geometry stop select earliest
  PMT with slot tie-break; a physical ray deposits at most one PMT per leg.
  Cylinder outward normals and negative PMT radial normals have the correct
  convention for the shared specular formula and cosine reflection sampler.

Coverage limits
  No claim of exact real-detector agreement or exhaustive proof follows from
  these analytic/random checks. Real optical calibration is another audit area.
  Endpoint search and spherical PMTs are accepted approximations, not findings.
  Unused single-ray output squeezing and unified box/cap helpers are outside
  SK_WAND batch production. Outside-emission suppression and raw input units
  were traced to simulator/source glue but are assigned to other audit agents.
