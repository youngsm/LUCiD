Requested after the initial LUCiD audit, 6 October 2026

All builds, imports, numerical analysis and simulations must use Slurm milano

Coverage assignments and physical questions

transport/: PhysicsList.cc and actual constructed Geant4 process managers.
  Are all relevant electromagnetic, hadronic, stopping and decay channels
  present? Compare process construction, energy/species coverage, interaction
  rates and muon endpoint behavior to physical references and complete lists.

cherenkov/: DetectorConstruction.cc water properties and actual G4Cerenkov.
  Independent dispersive Frank--Tamm yield, threshold, spectrum, cone angle,
  beta dependence, material composition/density and production-cut convergence.

emission/: SteppingAction.cc, StackingAction.cc, DataManager.cc, EventAction.cc,
  RunAction.cc and their headers/messengers. Compare photon creation snapshots
  to first-step recording and ROOT arrays. Check loss/duplication, timestamps,
  directions, wavelength, chunk boundaries, event resets and storage modes.

pion_replacement/: Artificial >5-degree pion kill-and-replace behavior.
  Compare physical endpoint/yield/range statistics with splitting disabled;
  check momentum, time, energy, lifetime and process-state semantics. The prior
  zero endpoint-position gap is not sufficient to establish equivalence.

primaries/: PrimaryGeneratorAction/Messenger, RooTrackerReader,
  RandomSeedMessenger, PhotonSim.cc and LUCiD generate_macro/config routing.
  Independently verify primary mixtures, multiplicity, energy/momentum/units,
  angular correlations, seeds, input completeness and failure behavior.

boundary/: Giant homogeneous source world followed by LUCiD translation and
  optical-source clipping. Test escaped/re-entering charged particles and
  secondary production near the ID boundary; distinguish geometry/material
  model uncertainty from demonstrated production errors.

Evidence and exclusions

Earlier confirmed spin-correlation and photon segment-label defects remain
valid; this extension should not count them as newly discovered bugs.
The user's accepted endpoint PMT search, spherical PMTs, late-light cuts,
constant LUCiD light speed, unpolarized optical scattering, QE wavelength
clamping, SPE shape, and named off-production issues remain excluded.
The user explicitly confirmed that SK_WAND particle-bomb/single-particle
configurations AND GENIE inputs are production scope. GENIE input failures and
lost final-state information belong in the main findings. Synthetic fixtures
establish reachability, not their frequency in the user's actual datasets.
No generic claim of 100% detector agreement can follow from finite tests or
from agreement between two Geant4 configurations. Every finding needs an
independent physical expectation, executed minimal witness and bounded impact;
model/calibration uncertainties must remain explicit.
