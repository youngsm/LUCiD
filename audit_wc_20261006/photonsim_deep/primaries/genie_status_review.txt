GENIE final-state status and nuclear-remnant review
2026-10-06, client date. Read-only source/binary/header/config inspection.
No GENIE event generation, executable tests, imports, or builds performed.

Conclusion
  Reject the broad claim that PhotonSim's status==1 selection discards
  GENIE's explicitly generated oxygen nuclear-deexcitation photons.
  GENIE creates those photons as status 1, retains them through intranuclear
  transport, and ordinary rootracker exports their status unchanged.

  Distinguish this from two other cases:
  (a) Status 15 is normally an aggregate residual hadronic-state entry for
      the configured HAIntranuke2018 chain, not a ready-to-propagate ion.
      Its unresolved recoil/excitation is a generator/interface modelling
      limitation; accepting status 15 as a Geant4 particle is not a fix.
  (b) Real status-1 ions do occur in coherent-pion production. PhotonSim's
      FindParticle-only lookup can skip them. This establishes source
      reachability of omitted nuclear recoil, not its production frequency,
      detector light yield, or a large missing-deexcitation signal.

1. Export semantics: source, not merely enum comments
  R-3_04_00 gNtpConv.cxx:2011 sets hide_truth only for rootracker_mock_data.
  Lines 2367-2398 iterate event particles. Line 2374 filters non-status-1
  particles only if hide_truth; line 2377 directly assigns p->Status() to
  StdHepStatus. Pdg, P4, X4, polarization and ancestry are copied as well.
  Ordinary '-f rootracker' therefore retains status 15 and intermediate
  entries in the exported record; the later PhotonSim reader applies the
  status-1 filter. R-3_06_02 retains this behavior (status assignment 2369).

  PhotonSim RooTrackerReader.cc:99 selects status==1, after converting
  momenta/energy GeV to MeV. PrimaryGeneratorAction.cc then resolves each
  particle using G4ParticleTable::FindParticle(p.pdg); unresolved PDGs are
  skipped. It supplies the three-momentum to G4PrimaryParticle.

  Source:
    https://github.com/GENIE-MC/Generator/blob/R-3_04_00/src/Apps/gNtpConv.cxx#L2374

2. Explicit oxygen deexcitation photons survive the status filter
  R-3_04_00 NucDeExcitationSim.cxx:76 dispatches oxygen targets to its oxygen
  model. AddPhoton at 486-514 creates a gamma with kIStStableFinalState
  (line 506), appends it (507), and subtracts its four-momentum from the
  remnant (510-513). Thus its photon and residual-state energy are already
  distinct contributions, not two independent copies of excitation energy.
  R-3_06_02 has the same relevant construction/subtraction.

  Active R-3_04_00 QE CC EventGenerator.xml:154-165 runs NucDeExcitationSim
  at 161, then HadronTransporter at 162; QE NC does so at 221-222. Do not
  mistake earlier commented alternative module lists for the active list.
  G18_10a/ModelConfiguration.xml:59-60 enables HAIntranuke2018. That class
  inherits Intranuke2018 (HAIntranuke2018.h:51). In neutrino mode,
  Intranuke2018::NeedsRescattering (217-233) processes only status 14, so it
  leaves explicit status-1 deexcitation gammas alone.

  This verifies the routing of photons the generator actually emits. It
  does not establish that the generator emits every physically required
  transition, has accurate branching ratios, or fully models post-FSI
  excitation and fragmentation.

3. Status-15 remnant is not an ordinary status-1 isotope
  Intranuke2018.cxx:381-383 constructs its residual entry using
  kPdgHadronicBlob, kIStFinalStateNuclearRemnant, and fRemnP4. It then marks
  the prior remnant ion kIStIntermediateState for neutrino interactions
  (387-389). PDGCodes.h defines kPdgHadronicBlob as 2000000002, and GHepStatus.h
  defines nuclear-remnant status as 15 and intermediate status as 2.
  The same operations appear in the preserved R-3_06_02 source.

  The source preserves leftover four-momentum as a pseudo-particle rather
  than exporting a complete residual-isotope/excitation/fragment state that
  PhotonSim can hand directly to Geant4. Simply importing the prior ion or
  treating all blob energy as photons risks inconsistency or double counting
  the already emitted particles. Further residual deexcitation would require
  an explicit, validated generator-to-detector model and suitable state
  information. No such model or physical yield was validated here.

  Source:
    https://github.com/GENIE-MC/Generator/blob/R-3_04_00/src/Physics/HadronTransport/Intranuke2018.cxx#L378

4. Narrow source-reachable ion omission: coherent nuclear recoil
  R-3_04_00 COHHadronicSystemGenerator.cxx explicitly appends the recoil
  nucleus with the target's ion PDG and kIStStableFinalState. Examples:
    BergerSehgalFM: recoil four-vector at 197-200, AddParticle at 207.
    ReinSehgal: recoil four-vector at 306-309, AddParticle at 315.
    AlvarezRuso: recoil four-vector at 355-358, AddParticle at 366.
  Thus a real status-1 oxygen ion is possible in upstream output. This is
  distinct from the post-intranuclear-cascade status-15 blob described above.

  PhotonSim's unknown-PDG skip can omit this recoil if the ion has not been
  registered in G4ParticleTable. Registration can depend on process history:
  earlier hadronic interactions can create ion definitions. A primary-only
  fresh-process fixture cannot establish omission frequency in a full run.
  A narrow integration improvement is to
  resolve supported nuclear PDGs through G4IonTable, with explicit validation
  of the incoming ion state, and to diagnose unsupported status-1 particles
  instead of silently skipping them. This does not fix unresolved blob
  excitation or demonstrate a large Cherenkov-light change.

  A synthetic status-1 oxygen fixture tests the lookup/fallback mechanism.
  It does not establish that its energy/momentum, rate, or excitation state
  represents this coherent channel or normal GENIE production. No actual
  GENIE event sample was available to this reviewer. Do not infer physical
  representativeness or quantitative missing light from that fixture.

  Source:
    https://github.com/GENIE-MC/Generator/blob/R-3_04_00/src/Physics/Coherent/EventGen/COHHadronicSystemGenerator.cxx#L197
