1. HIGH-PRIORITY STOPPED NEGATIVE MUON HYPOTHESIS

Physical oracle:
- Negative muons in pure water: mean disappearance lifetime 1.7954 +/- 0.0020 us.
- Nuclear capture probability 0.184 +/- 0.001; decay probability about 0.816.
The SK Kitagawa 2022 thesis states both on printed pp.63-64 (PDF pp.72-73),
citing Suzuki, Measday and Roalsvig, PRC 35, 2212 (1987):
https://www-sk.icrr.u-tokyo.ac.jp/sk/_pdf/articles/2022/Master_Thesis_Kitagawa.ver8_.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.35.2212
The 1987 full text could not be fetched with web tools; exact numbers were
read in the institutional thesis, not guessed from the primary abstract.

Decisive independent physical mechanism:
Super-Kamiokande, H. Kitagawa et al., PRD 110, 082008 (2024), printed p.9:
muonic hydrogen diffuses in water and transfers the muon to oxygen on a
timescale much shorter than decay. The paper therefore treats decay in orbit
in water as oxygen-bound. This is not a negligible isotope/thermal-neutron
approximation. The same paper discusses oxygen DIO energy-spectrum distortion.
https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/340602/1/340602.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.082008

but oxygen by number density times 0.56*Z. For H2O, H:O weights are 2:4.48.
Therefore about31% of its selected atomic captures are on H. Lines99-114 choose

fact; the physical problem depends on whether a later transfer step exists.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4HadronStoppingProcess.cc
AtRestDoIt lines130-158 selects target once, performs its EM atomic cascade,
then directly applies bound decay/capture to that same target. Afterward it
creates final daughters and kills the muon. No intervening material transfer.

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4EmCaptureCascade.cc

uses rapid-transfer approximation for WAND, not a molecular-cascade simulation.

Oracle limitations (our test-design deductions):
CSDA is integrated stopping path length, not exact endpoint displacement or
the unconditioned range of a proton sample with nuclear interactions enabled.
For proton stopping-power checks use an EM-only diagnostic or no-inelastic

and nuclear interactions prevent requiring bitwise equality to tabulations.

3. MISSING GAMMA/ELECTRON/POSITRON NUCLEAR INTERACTIONS

PhotonSim registers EM option4 and hadron constructor QGSP_BERT, but no
G4EmExtraPhysics. The full reference QGSP_BERT list includes photo-nuclear and
lepto-nuclear interactions; merely registering its hadron inelastic constructor
does not establish those processes exist. Parent owns actual process dump/A-B.
Official physics-list description:
https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/dev/plg_html/PhysicsListGuide/reference_PL/QGSP_BERT.html

Primary data supporting real prompt effect:
Burgov et al., Soviet Physics JETP16(1963)50, measured oxygen gamma absorption
using distilled water in18.9-26.6MeV. Nuclear absorption contributes a few
percent of total absorption and has peaks near22.3,23.05,24.3,25.15MeV.
https://www.jetp.ras.ru/cgi-bin/dn/e_016_01_0050.pdf
This motivates25MeV gamma A/B and low-light-tail comparison; it does not prove
any particular average PE bias for WAND without an actual production spectrum.

Evaluated primary institutional data:
IAEA-TECDOC1178, oxygen evaluation printedpp102-103 (PDFpp107-108), gives
oxygen16 thresholds: gamma,n15.66MeV; gamma,p12.13MeV; gamma,alpha7.16MeV.
It uses measured Ahrens absorption and separate direct ground-state channels;
below30MeV direct gamma,n0 and gamma,p0 contributions matter. The evaluation
also documents limitations of statistical nuclear modeling for light nuclei.
https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/publications/PDF/te_1178_prn.pdf
Primary Ahrens measurement DOI:10.1016/0375-9474(75)90543-6.

Why relevant even without neutron tagging (deduction):
Photonuclear conversion can remove energy from visible electromagnetic
secondaries immediately, change prompt deexcitation gamma content, and produce
charged hadrons. The parent gamma may be primary or arise in an electron/positron
shower. A late-neutron acceptance policy does not remove this prompt effect.
Report event-rate/tail/mean separately. Secondary nuclear processes being rare
does not imply every affected event has a small change.

4. MODEL VALIDATION LIMITS

The root reviewer notes Geant4 documentation says a Bertini11.2 compatibility
switch was added in11.3.2 because11.3 changes did not consistently improve
thin-target comparisons. The pinned11.3.0 model therefore has version-specific
hadron uncertainties. This is a reason to measure against data, not proof of a
PhotonSim defect or permission to claim a model is universally exact.
https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/dev/plg_html/PhysicsListGuide/physicslistguide.html

No claim of universal physical correctness follows from constructor coverage,
correct energy conservation, or one alternative-Geant4 agreement. The physical
water capture/lifetime test above can falsify a standard-model configuration
even when all its classes behave exactly as implemented.
