Independent review: negative-particle stopping targets in water
Reviewed 2026-10-06 by the production-integrity lane.

Conclusion
The proposed water-target defect is supported independently by the experimental
definition and the exact Geant4 11.3.0 stopping-process code. The default element
selector's hydrogen choice is not merely an innocuous initial atomic-capture
choice: the inspected stopping process carries that same nucleus through its
EM cascade and terminal bound decay/nuclear interaction. Use separate physical
endpoint prescriptions for negative pions and negative muons. The current
WaterPionElementSelector.hh correctly preserves the measured finite hydrogen
fraction and guards both particle identity and water material.

This is a read-only source/literature review. I ran no duplicate simulation,
import, build, or numerical program. Runtime rates and photon effects must be
taken from the transport agent's allowed-partition ledger/A-B experiments.

Experimental definition: pions
Berridge et al., Phys. Rev. A 75, 034501 (2007), DOI:
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.75.034501
Publisher abstract:
https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.75.034501
Author-posted full text, including definition and methods:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/242310160_Study_ofNuclear_Capture_Ratio_on_Hydrogen_and_Oxygen_in_Water

The full text defines the quantity as the fraction of stopped negative pions
captured on hydrogen nuclei in a compound. Thus W_H2O=(4.45 +/- 0.24)*10^-3 is
a final nuclear-capture probability, not the probability of initially forming
pionic hydrogen, not a per-hydrogen-atom probability, and not the radiative
branching fraction. Their pi0 method counts pi0->gamma gamma, corrects for the
competing radiative hydrogen channel using the Panofsky ratio, and normalizes
to pion stops. Their independent gamma-spectrum method separates hydrogen and
oxygen contributions. In-flight charge exchange backgrounds were subtracted.
The stated consistency across several beam momenta concerns pions stopping in
the water target. This supports a stopping-target oracle; it does not set the
fraction of all incident GeV pions interacting on hydrogen while moving.

Keep the nonzero hydrogen branch. An oxygen-only pion prescription is a useful
diagnostic but removes a measured physical channel. The measurement does not
establish a complete molecular-transfer model; this fix is an empirical final-
target distribution for liquid water.

Experimental applicability: muons
Kitagawa et al. (Super-Kamiokande), Phys. Rev. D 110, 082008 (2024), printed p.9:
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.082008
https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/340602/1/340602.pdf
The paper explicitly describes muonic hydrogen diffusing in water and moving
its muon to oxygen much faster than muon decay. It consequently models water
decay-in-orbit as oxygen-bound. Printed p.12 cites measured water nuclear
capture probability 0.184 +/- 0.001. This supports an oxygen endpoint for a
minimal rapid-transfer muon approximation. Do not apply the pion's 0.00445
hydrogen probability to muons. Conversely, forcing pion endpoints to oxygen
because the muon approximation uses oxygen is unjustified.

Exact implementation chain and absence of an intervening transfer stage
Local preserved files below are under this directory's reference/.

1. PhotonSim upstream/src/PhysicsList.cc registers G4StoppingPhysics(0).
   upstream/src/DetectorConstruction.cc constructs actual G4_WATER through the
   NIST material manager; this is the material targeted by the audit selectors.

2. G4StoppingPhysics.cc constructs a G4MuonMinusCapture and registers it on mu-.
   It constructs one G4HadronicAbsorptionBertini object and registers that same
   object on pi-, K-, Sigma-, Xi-, and Omega- when applicable. This shared object
   is why a pion-only selector must check the current track's PDG. Merely finding
   the process via the pion process manager does not make it pion-exclusive.
   Official pinned source:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/physics_lists/constructors/stopping/src/G4StoppingPhysics.cc

3. G4HadronStoppingProcess.cc constructs its default G4ElementSelector and
   G4EmCaptureCascade. In AtRestDoIt it calls SelectZandA once, invokes the EM
   cascade with that nucleus, invokes any bound-decay handler with that nucleus,
   and passes the same nucleus to ChooseHadronicInteraction and ApplyYourself.
   It then kills the original track and creates daughters. There is no atomic
   track handed to another transport process between selection and terminal
   interaction. At-rest interaction length is zero; muon disappearance time is
   sampled internally by its bound-decay model.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4HadronStoppingProcess.cc

4. G4ElementSelector.cc weights element number densities by Z, except oxygen's
   0.56*Z multiplier and a separate halogen multiplier. H2O therefore has H:O
   weights 2:4.48, so the selected hydrogen probability is about 0.309. This is
   a source-derived probability, not a runtime measurement in this review.
   The chosen element's isotope is then sampled and written to the nucleus.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4ElementSelector.cc

5. Exact G4HadronicAbsorptionBertini.cc calls the base stopping constructor,
   registers a G4CascadeInterface, enables its zero-energy range and precompound
   deexcitation, and does not replace the selector or EM cascade. Its installed
   header has no AtRestDoIt override. The preserved source arrived through the
   transport lane and was independently read here.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4HadronicAbsorptionBertini.cc

6. Exact G4EmCaptureCascade::ApplyYourself reads target Z/A, calculates atomic
   level transitions, emits Auger electrons/photons, and returns. It never
   updates target Z/A or chooses another element. Reading the entire function
   rules out transfer hidden inside the EM cascade.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4EmCaptureCascade.cc

7. G4MuonMinusCapture.cc registers G4MuonMinusBoundDecay; the latter reads Z/A
   from the selected target and immediately samples its capture/decay rates and
   lifetime. It does not change target before that choice. Thus later nuclear
   final-state modeling cannot rescue the muon capture/decay branching ratio.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4MuonMinusCapture.cc
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/stopping/src/G4MuonMinusBoundDecay.cc

8. The subsequently provided exact G4CascadeInterface.cc closes the target-
   reselection question at the Bertini interface: ApplyYourself calls
   createTarget(theNucleus), branches on target A==1, and retries collisions
   against the same target. createTarget extracts precisely the supplied A/Z.
   There is no material-dependent replacement with an oxygen nucleus. The
   transport agent's target ledger plus pi0/hard-gamma counts supplies the
   runtime check of the corresponding hydrogen-like final states. This review
   does not claim validation of every Bertini nuclear final-state model or any
   measured photon-yield number.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Geant4/geant4/v11.3.0/source/processes/hadronic/models/cascade/cascade/src/G4CascadeInterface.cc

Minimal material-aware correction
Use G4HadronStoppingProcess::SetElementSelector, which is an exposed supported
hook in the installed 11.3.0 header. Retain default behavior outside the known
pure-water material, and preserve conditional isotope selection.

For pi- in pure water, select desired Z once with Bernoulli(0.00445): H if true,
otherwise O. Then sample the isotope conditional on the selected element. The
current audit implementation does this by repeatedly calling the base selector
until it returns the already chosen Z. This preserves the base isotope law.
Do not redraw the desired element in the rejection loop. Check PDG -211 because
the Bertini stopping process is shared with other particles. The existing
WaterPionElementSelector.hh satisfies both conditions.

For mu- in pure water, choose O, preserving oxygen isotope abundances. Attach
only to G4MuonMinusCapture, or also guard PDG 13 inside a reusable selector.
The existing WaterMuonElementSelector.hh is safe under its present attachment
to the exclusively-muon G4MuonMinusCapture. A production implementation should
prefer known material identity/composition over an unconstrained material-name
string, and verify the requested elements are present before rejection loops.
Do not apply either fix globally to all H/O mixtures, heavy water, loaded water,
other hydrides, or other stopped particle species without the relevant data.

These are minimal phenomenological endpoint corrections. They do not reproduce
the microscopic atomic/molecular cascade, transfer delay, or initial-H x-ray
history, nor validate all oxygen nuclear final-state spectra. State those limits
instead of calling the resulting simulation exactly physical in every detail.

Interpretation cautions for the final report
* Compare pion hydrogen fraction per stopped capture, not per generated GeV
  pion. In-flight interactions/decays dilute effects in the incident sample.
* Report selected-target discrepancy separately from measured created photons,
  transported detector photons and digitized PE. Their percentages are different.
* Negative-muon nuclear capture and DIO share muMinusCaptureAtRest as creator
  process. Classify daughters with creator-model identity/ancestry, not a simple
  creator-process-name == Decay requirement; avoid counting atomic Auger e- as
  Michel electrons.
* The 10 us/100 us accepted late cuts do not remove this physical target issue:
  pion capture changes prompt products, and muon decay/capture probability is
  wrong before any late-light selection.

Residual radiative-capture validation required after the target correction
Spuller et al., Phys. Lett. B 67 (1977) 479-482, measured stopped-pion hydrogen
charge-exchange/radiative ratio P=1.546 +/- 0.009:
https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(77)90449-X
Primary-paper copy:
https://muon.npl.washington.edu/exp/WildIdeas/DarkLiterature/piHe/spuller77.pdf
Neglecting much smaller branches, P(pi0 n | H)=P/(1+P) and
P(gamma n | H)=1/(1+P), approximately 61% and 39%. The transport agent reports
pi0 daughters in every simulated H capture and no direct hard gamma. Thus
correcting H/O selection alone does not validate the conditional H final state.
After imposing the small measured hydrogen probability, this residual affects
only that small fraction of water stops; do not confuse it with the much larger
original H/O target mistake. A suitable additional H fix must sample both
channels with two-body kinematics and energy conservation, not append a gamma
to an already generated pi0 event.

Bistirlich et al., Phys. Rev. C 5, 1867 (1972), measured radiative stopped-pion
photon spectra on O16 (and other nuclei) in the 50-160 MeV range:
https://journals.aps.org/prc/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevC.5.1867
Berridge's gamma-fit method uses an oxygen radiative-capture normalization of
2.27 +/- 0.24%. Its fit begins at 50 MeV. I have not independently resolved
the original normalization's integration threshold, so do not promote 2.27%
to an all-energy branching oracle from this review alone. The primary measured
spectrum does establish that a zero hard-gamma channel is not physically exact.
Oxygen capture spectral/channel validation remains with the transport lane.
