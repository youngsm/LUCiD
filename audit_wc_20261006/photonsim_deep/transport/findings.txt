CONFIRMED LARGE BUG: wrong target nucleus for stopped pi- in water
---------------------------------------------------------------
G4ElementSelector::SelectZandA weights hydrogen byZ*n, oxygen by0.56*Z*n.
ForH2O this assigns2/(2+0.56*8)=30.86% toH. The selected nucleus passes
unchanged through G4HadronStoppingProcess and G4CascadeInterface.
Measured final H nuclear-capture probability is0.00445+/-0.00024, from
Berridge etal PRA75,034501(2007), DOI10.1103/PhysRevA.75.034501.
This is a final nuclear-capture fraction, already Panofsky corrected, not
merely the initial atomic-capture probability.

Executed3000 cold pi-:
baselineH880/3000=29.33%; fixedH19/3000=0.633%.
Every H capture produced one pi0; no oxygen capture produced a pi0.
Mean Cherenkov photons9435.45+/-243.12 ->1079.61+/-45.99 (baseline8.74x).
Executed1000 monoenergetic pi- at200MeV (gun production config lower bound,
not a GENIE-wide bound or weighted-distribution measurement):
baseline497 stopped captures,162 onH; fixed502 stopped captures,2 onH.
Mean Cherenkov24807.81+/-601.12 ->18603.74+/-515.18, a25.0% reduction.
This is a large prompt shower/topology error, not neutron tagging or late light.

Minimal target-selection fix: WaterPionElementSelector.hh, installed using
minimal_water_capture_registration.cc, samplesH with0.00445 andO otherwise,
retaining natural isotope selection. BOTH species(PDG-211) andG4_WATER guards
are required: G4StoppingPhysics shares one Bertini process among pi-/K-/hyperons.
The registration snippet compile-checks; the corresponding isolated runtime
variant passes the measured target-fraction test. This fixes target selection
only, not the separate missing radiative final states discussed below.

CONFIRMED BUG: wrong mu- capture/decay mixture in water
----------------------------------------------------
The same selector leaves~31% mu- endpoints onH, despite muonic-H transfer toO
before disappearance in water. SK PRD110,082008(2024), SecIII.2, explicitly
states this transfer and reports water capture18.4+/-0.1%, lifetime1795.4+/-2.0ns:
https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/340602/1/340602.pdf

Executed10000 mu- at1e-6MeV, using actual positive-KE stopping:
baseline capture12.86+/-0.335%, lifetime1926.5+/-19.4ns;
water-only oxygen selector18.17+/-0.386%,1814.9+/-18.1ns.
Baseline fails capture by15.86sigma and lifetime6.73sigma; fixed passes.
Tracked Michel daughters above1MeV before10us change86.49% ->81.53%;
this is not a PMT visibility or tagging efficiency measurement.
Created Cherenkov6965.84 ->6638.44; neutrons/event0.2154 ->0.3150.
Independent200MeV mu- samples2000 each also fail baseline/pass water oracle.
The mu+ control giveszero capture and2224.6+/-22.3ns, consistent2196.9811ns.
Stopped-only denominators exclude the rare in-flight decay at200MeV.

Minimal fix: WaterMuonElementSelector.hh redirects only G4_WATER muon
capture tooxygen, preserving natural isotopes, via the supported
G4MuonMinusCapture::SetElementSelector hook. It represents fast transfer by
choosing the final capture nucleus before the atomic cascade; a full atomic
transfer model would additionally represent microscopic cascade/transfer
details. Switching to G4MuonMinusAtomicCapture alone does not fix this: it
uses the same generic selector.

CONFIRMED RESIDUAL: missing radiative pion capture final states
------------------------------------------------------------
All880 baseline Hcaptures madepi0n, zero hardcapturegamma. Exact
G4CascadeT31piNChannel.cc two-body table containspi-p,pi0n,K0Lambda,K0Sigma0,
K+Sigma- andNOgamma-n; pi0n is the sole allowed inelastic state atrest.
Panofsky ratioP=1.546 requires electromagneticcapture-family probability
1/(1+P)~39.3% onH.
Precision: this ratio groups the radiative family (realgamma orinternal
conversion) againstthepi0 family. Treatingall39.3%asrealgamma is a
leading-channel approximation; physicalcompleteness also needs conversion.
After targetfix this affects~0.175% ofallwater pionstops, rather than the
much larger~31% currently incorrectly assigned toH.
Also zero directcapturegamma>10MeV in3000 stopped-pion fixedevents,
including2981 oxygencaptures. Primary measured oxygen radiative photons
50-160MeV exist (Bistirlich etal PRC5,1867(1972),
DOI10.1103/PhysRevC.5.1867; Strassner etal PRC20,248(1979),
DOI10.1103/PhysRevC.20.248).
Bistirlich's full author manuscript gives oxygen totalbranch2.24+/-0.48%,
including extrapolation below50MeV; it must not be described as a measured
>50MeV branch. Independent of that extrapolation, measured128.10MeV gamma
ground-state branch0.15+/-0.03% and giant-resonance branch0.25+/-0.06%
(117-121MeV) together predict~12 hard photons in2981 O captures, but zero
are generated. The rates share normalization uncertainty; using the sum
of their quoted errors as a conservative bound, lowering by3 such bounds
leaves approximately2.1% probability to seezero. Fullradiative continuum
adds a larger missing contribution. Primary author manuscript:
https://escholarship.org/content/qt5835t658/qt5835t658.pdf printedp24.
test_radiative_oracle.py records this explicit residual physical failure.
The target selector is therefore not a complete physicalcapturefix.
Minimal H correction can use a dedicated pi- G4HadronStoppingProcess with
custom G4HadronicInteraction: forA=1,Z=1 choosegamma+n with1/(1+1.546)
orpi0+n otherwise, using exacttwo-body recoil with
W=m_pi+m_p-projectile.GetBoundEnergy(). Remove old sharedBertini onlyfrom
thepi- manager; do notdelete a process stillsharedbykaons/hyperons.
Othernuclei can delegate toG4CascadeInterface. The O correction needs a
radiativecapture final-state model with measuredline/continuumspectrum
and consistent recoiling residualnucleus/de-excitation, for example
pi-+16O ->gamma+16N* followedbyappropriate residualdecay.
Adding anunbalancedgamma on top of anexistingcascade is notadequate.
Neither radiativefinalstate fix was implemented or claimed validated.
