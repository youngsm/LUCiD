PhotonSim pion kill/replacement: Geant4 11.3.0 static semantics review
==================================================================

Scope: upstream/src/SteppingAction.cc lines 106-174. Read-only source audit;
no tests, builds, imports, or numerical scripts executed by this reviewer.
No production changes. Population A/B tests belong to the parent audit.

Disposition
-----------
No large physical failure is established by resetting the track alone.
Do not report a pion lifetime-extension bug, a fSuspend lost-track bug, or
an Urban multiple-scattering restart bug based only on this code. These
specific hypotheses are contradicted by the actual production model and
Geant4 lifecycle. Distribution-level A/B evidence could reveal another
mechanism; different individual seeded histories are expected and are
not themselves evidence of physical bias.

1. Status, geometry, and already-produced secondaries
---------------------------------------------------
G4TrackingManager::ProcessOneTrack calls SetInitialStep before its tracking
loop and StartTracking before stepping. G4SteppingManager::SetInitialStep
turns fSuspend/fPostponeToNextEvent into fAlive, overrides zero KE to
fStopButAlive, and locates tracks whose touchable handle is empty. Thus
ordinary moving replacements resume and stopped replacements invoke the
at-rest process machinery. A missing touchable does not by itself lose a
track inside homogeneous water. Boundary relocation remains an empirical
edge check, not a confirmed bug; the replacement trigger excludes steps
whose defining process is Transportation.

Sources:
https://github.com/Geant4/geant4/blob/v11.3.0/source/tracking/src/G4TrackingManager.cc#L54
https://github.com/Geant4/geant4/blob/v11.3.0/source/tracking/src/G4SteppingManager.cc#L248

G4EventManager retains and stacks secondaries for fStopAndKill. Only
fKillTrackAndSecondaries destroys the secondary list. PhotonSim uses
fStopAndKill, so already-produced Cerenkov photons, recoil nuclei, and
delta electrons survive replacement. The manually appended pion is
assigned a new track ID when the secondaries are stacked.

Source:
https://github.com/Geant4/geant4/blob/v11.3.0/source/event/src/G4EventManager.cc#L231

G4Cerenkov::PostStepDoIt proposes fSuspend, and only when its incoming
status is fAlive. It does not produce fSuspendAndWait. The latter status
exists in 11.3 and is normally converted to fSuspend when placed into a
waiting stack. PhotonSim's unconditional urgent stacking would deserve
attention if another process generated fSuspendAndWait; no such producer
was found in PhotonSim. Do not present that hypothetical as production.

2. Decay and interaction clocks: memorylessness matters
-----------------------------------------------------
G4Decay::StartTracking resets the sampled remaining interaction length.
For ordinary pions (no preassigned decay time), subsequent decay GPIL uses
the remaining exponential clock and current momentum/mass; it does not
decay them by comparing accumulated track proper time to their lifetime.
G4VProcess::ResetNumberOfInteractionLengthLeft samples -log(U).

The residual of a surviving exponential clock has exactly the same law
as a fresh exponential clock: P(T>s+t | T>s)=exp(-t/tau). Resampling after
an independently generated deflection therefore does not automatically
extend the physical lifetime distribution. The same argument applies to
other Poisson interactions using integrated interaction length, including
energy-dependent cross sections when expressed in that hazard coordinate.

For a suspended ordinary track, ProcessOneTrack is called again on resume,
so the original unreplaced track would already call StartTracking again.
This substantially weakens any attribution of a new clock reset to the
replacement specifically in the fSuspend population.

3. Actual pion multiple-scattering model
--------------------------------------
G4EmStandardPhysics_option4 delegates charged hadrons to
G4EmBuilder::ConstructCharged(hmsc,pnuc). Its default isWVI=true and
ConstructLightHadrons installs G4WentzelVIModel for pi+/pi-. Wentzel's
StartTracking only sets up the particle; it has no Urban-style firstStep
or initial-range reset. Energy, material, range, lambda, and the
single-scattering-mode choice are recomputed for each step.

Urban has additional first-step state, but that model is not the pion
model in this production physics list. Do not generalize its behavior.
The energy-loss process resets caches/discrete clocks; its continuous
step limit uses current energy and range, not elapsed track age.

4. Real state loss, but no demonstrated production physics consequence
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Fresh G4Track/G4DynamicParticle construction resets local/proper time,
weight, user information, original touchable, preassigned decay data,
and dynamic particle properties to constructor defaults. Position,
global time, pion identity, and post-step three-momentum are retained.
Charged pions have spin zero, so polarization reset is immaterial here.

Preassigned decay time/products would be a real hazard: such a pion's
decay must honor that assignment rather than draw a fresh exponential.
No PhotonSim primary-generation path sets such assignments, custom
track weights, custom pion masses, or user track information. GENIE
primaries are constructed from particle definition and three-momentum;
other modes use G4ParticleGun. Therefore these are extension hazards,
not demonstrated production findings.

5. Concrete first-step labeling issue, separate from physical transport
---------------------------------------------------------------------
In PhotonSim, step 1 registers preMomentumDir from the vertex direction,
then bypasses BOTH the deflection check and UpdatePionMomentum. Therefore
step 2 compares its post-step direction against the original vertex
direction, not necessarily the immediately preceding step direction.
A first-step deflection can be missed or credited to a later hIoni/
hadElastic step; the first step after each replacement has the same blind
spot. This changes split labels and initial track summaries. It does not
remove the already-simulated physical deflection or emitted photons.

Minimal correction to investigate if label fidelity is in scope: use
step->GetPreStepPoint()->GetMomentumDirection() for the per-step angle,
and permit a split on step 1 after registration. A broader cleaner
architecture records kinks as metadata without replacing physical tracks.
Do not fold this bookkeeping issue into a claim of lost pion light.

Recommended instrumentation for parent A/B
----------------------------------------
Record replacement status, KE, global/proper/local time, preassigned decay
time presence, dynamic-vs-PDG mass, weight, and volume; count which fresh
tracks reach their first step. Compare total pion-chain light, decay vs
absorption frequencies, stopping times, range, and daughter light across
ensembles. Treat changed RNG consumption as expected. No new population
tests were run by this reviewer.

Addendum: G4VMultipleScattering wrapper checked directly
------------------------------------------------------
The exact v11.3.0 wrapper has no per-track first-step flag, age counter,
or accumulated range state. StartTracking (actual source lines 199-214)
updates the particle-species/ionisation-process cache and calls each
model's StartTracking. AlongStepGetPhysicalInteractionLength ignores its
previousStepSize argument and overwrites physStepLimit, gPathLength and
tPathLength from the current proposed step. It selects the model from
current KE/material and assigns isActive on both branches. AlongStepDoIt
initializes from the current step, resets fPositionChanged, and computes
the true step and displacement anew. Its class members were also checked;
firstParticle names a table-initialization species, not a first-step flag.
G4ParticleChangeForMSC::InitialiseMSC copies the current status, post-step
position/direction, and track pointer; no hidden first-step branch exists.

One numerical distinction remains outside wrapper track-history state:
G4Step::InitializeStep sets pre-step safety=0 and status=fUndefined. A
continuing unsuspended track can instead carry a conservative positive
safety from the previous step. Wentzel recomputes a small safety when the
step is not fGeomBoundary; its range<safety early exit means a different
safety cache can change finite-step partitioning. G4SafetyHelper caches by
position, not by track identity, and recomputes via the navigator when the
position differs. Therefore exact next-step equality is NOT established
solely by memorylessness. No resulting distribution bias is demonstrated.
For fSuspend, ordinary resumption already calls InitializeStep, so this
fresh-step initialization is not introduced uniquely by replacement.
