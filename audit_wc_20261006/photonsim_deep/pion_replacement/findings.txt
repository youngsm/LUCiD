Pion deflection track replacement: no established consequential physics bug
==========================================================================

Scope
-----
PhotonSim upstream/src/SteppingAction.cc:106-174 kills a pi+/pi- track after
a >5-degree hadElastic/hIoni deflection and appends a same-species child at
the post-step position/global time/three-momentum, retaining status. This
audit isolates the physical effect of that operation. It does NOT validate
the absolute correctness of Geant4 capture targets or hadronic models;
those are identical in both variants and belong to separate audit tasks.

Executed comparison
-------------------
Slurm milano job 39971593, sdfmilan272, COMPLETED 0:0, elapsed 5m01s.
Geant4 11.3.0, the frozen PhotonSim source and its actual physics list,
four independent single-thread processes inside a 4-CPU allocation.
No production source was edited. The isolated on/off SteppingAction
copies differ in exactly one branch:

    - if (angle > 5.0 * deg) {
    + if (false && angle > 5.0 * deg) {

2,800 events per variant: 300 each for pi+/pi- at 200, 1000, 2000 MeV
kinetic energy, plus 500 each at 2 MeV to exercise stopped replacement.
Identical initial seeds; individual histories diverge because replacement
changes stacking/RNG consumption. Independent-ensemble standard errors
are reported; bitwise per-event agreement is not expected or asserted.
Individual photon ROOT storage was disabled in both variants; physical
optical transport continued and an identical instrumentation hook counted
every first optical-photon step, parent-chain light, ranges and daughters.

Deterministic invariants checked on every event
---------------------------------------------
All 2,645 replacement children in the on-variant resumed and were linked
to their actual old-track parent. This includes 1,517 fAlive, 518
fStopButAlive and 610 fSuspend replacements. No child was lost. Every
primary continuation chain had exactly one eventual physical termination.
Child birth position/global time matched the old endpoint exactly; largest
kinetic-energy reconstruction discrepancy was 6.8213e-13 MeV. The sum of
continuation-track counts was exactly one plus the replacement count.

Population results
------------------
Total optical-photon mean: replacement ON minus OFF, percentage of OFF.
Intervals below are approximate 95% intervals, not subpercent validation.

    pi+  200 MeV:  +1.612% +/-  9.735%
    pi+ 1000 MeV:  -0.818% +/-  7.617%
    pi+ 2000 MeV:  -1.088% +/-  6.199%
    pi-  200 MeV:  -0.853% +/- 12.199%
    pi- 1000 MeV:  +5.962% +/-  8.125%
    pi- 2000 MeV:  +1.849% +/-  6.586%

All production-energy photon changes are within 1.44 standard errors.
Pion-chain light, total chain range, terminal/max radius, terminal global
time, decay/capture/inelastic counts, muon daughters, other physical
daughters, and physical pion births show no established discrepancy.
The largest absolute mean z across 104 comparisons is 2.579 for the
2-MeV pi+ endpoint radius; this is not significant evidence after allowing
for the many comparisons and is not promoted to a bug. The 2-MeV pi+
sample decays in all 500 events in both variants (mean terminal times
27.984 vs 26.670 ns); stopped pi- replacements also reach normal at-rest
capture. These checks exclude the proposed lost-track/at-rest-loss
mechanisms without claiming the shared capture implementation is correct.

Code-grounded interpretation
---------------------------
See semantics.txt for exact Geant4 source links. SetInitialStep reactivates
suspended tracks, puts zero-KE tracks into at-rest processing and locates
missing touchables. fStopAndKill preserves already-created secondaries.
Ordinary pion decay uses an exponential interaction clock; restarting it
after survival does not itself extend lifetime. Production pions use
WentzelVI, and neither it nor the G4VMultipleScattering wrapper has the
suspected Urban first-step/track-age reset. Suspended tracks already call
StartTracking/InitializeStep on normal resume. A fresh step's conservative
geometry-safety cache can alter finite-step partitioning for fAlive tracks,
so exact numerical equivalence is not claimed. No aggregate physical bias
from that distinction is established by this comparison.

The first step skips updating preMomentumDir, so the next step may attach
a deflection label to the wrong step. This is a concrete bookkeeping
issue; no large physical-light consequence is shown and it is not a
physical bug finding. No physical fix is recommended from this evidence.

Reproduce and inspect
---------------------
From repository root, submit (do not execute numerical code on login):

    sbatch audit_wc_20261006/photonsim_deep/pion_replacement/run.sbatch

prepare.py generates the isolated source variants and event macros;
PionAudit.hh contains the measurement hooks; analyze.py checks invariants
and calculates uncertainties. summary.txt is the concise result and
results.json contains all statistics. CSVs preserve every event.
The earlier job 39971321 was canceled after an instrumentation correction
and is not evidence. Optional follow-up 39972370 was canceled while
pending (0 seconds); no additional statistical run was performed.

Limits
------
This comparison targets large changes under homogeneous-water transport,
ordinary production pion initial states and 200-2000 MeV kinetic energy.
It cannot establish 100% physical equivalence, rule out effects below its
confidence widths, validate absolute nuclear physics, or cover customized
weights/preassigned decay data that PhotonSim does not generate.
