Code and physical interpretation
--------------------------------
PhotonSim DetectorConstruction.hh lines 73-75 defaults to a 1000 m water box in
each dimension. DetectorConstruction.cc lines 382-398 instantiate that box.
LUCiD event_generation.py lines 468-475 rigidly translate photons in meters and
steps in millimeters, after the charged-particle shower has already been run.
simulator.py lines 599-602 gives photons born outside the optical detector zero
initial survival. This accepted outside-born-photon mask does not change the
charged-particle material history or manufacture missing external secondaries.

Rigid translation is physically consistent in homogeneous water. It cannot
provide a complete description of histories that depend on finite tank walls,
PMT/support solids, or concrete/rock. Nevertheless, killing every charged
particle at the ID cylinder would be an incorrect proposed fix: SK has an outer
water volume, and charged particles and secondaries can leave and re-enter the
ID while still propagating in water. The largest ID exposure above is therefore
not evidence of a bug, and it is explicitly not presented as one.

The real nominal tank is 39.3 m diameter and 41.4 m high. The optical ID is
smaller and is separated from OD water by an optically opaque structure, not by
the edge of the water tank. Detector material documentation describes thin
steel tank walls and concrete/rock outside the sides/bottom, but air and
equipment above the ceiling. A uniformly rock-backed cylinder would itself
be an uncontrolled simplification.

Limitations and fix scope if a future witness is found
----------------------------------------------------
The census follows only branches present in the homogeneous-water simulation.
It cannot bound new returning branches that steel, supports, concrete, or rock
would create. It does not certify optical leakage through the ID/OD separator,
PMT glass/support interactions, externally generated neutrons, or exact tank
shape/materials. No material-bounded comparison was run in this lane because
there was no full-tank return witness in the sampled source histories and a
detector-calibrated outside-material model is not available here. This is an
explicit unresolved detector-modelling boundary, not a reported catastrophic
bug or an established negligible effect for all possible events.

If a consequential material-dependent witness is established, the physical
correction belongs upstream: sample the final vertex before Geant4 transport
and transport non-optical particles through a finite water/OD/solid geometry
with supported materials. Merely masking outside-born optical photons, or
killing non-optical tracks at the ID, cannot reproduce secondary transport.
No minimal code fix is proposed without such a validated physical target.
