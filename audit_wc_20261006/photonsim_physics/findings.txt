CONFIRMED PHYSICS DEFECT: missing pion->muon->Michel spin correlation.

upstream/src/PhysicsList.cc:57 registers G4DecayPhysics but no spin decay.
Default G4MuonDecayChannel ignores muon spin. G4PionDecayMakeSpin and
G4DecayWithSpin are absent. This erases the joint angular distribution.
It is distinct from the accepted optical-photon unpolarized scattering.

Proof: pion_rest_baseline.mac and pion_rest_spin.mac each generate 2000
pi+ initially at rest, let the 4.1198 MeV mu+ stop in water and decay, and
record the positron and muon birth directions. Cerenkov alone is disabled
to eliminate photon storage; charged-particle transport and decay remain
the actual source physics. The accepted 10us cutoff is preserved.
Baseline: 1975 Michels, mean cosine +0.00845 +/- 0.01306, matching isotropy.
Spin constructor: 1980 Michels, -0.10155 +/- 0.01262, consistent with -1/9.
For positron kinetic energy >40MeV, fraction in same hemisphere as the
muon's birth momentum changes 0.52967 -> 0.33333.

Primary physical reference: SK collaboration, Kitagawa et al., PRD110
082008 (2024), Sec III.2 and III.3, https://arxiv.org/html/2403.08619v2 .
Their Michel formula has spin-dependent asymmetry and they use positive
muon polarization retention in water 0.718 +/- 0.007, so water does not
justify dropping this effect. Their negative-muon retention is much smaller.

Executed calibrated water variant: at the mu+ positive-energy->at-rest
transition in G4_WATER, preserve spin with probability0.718, otherwise set
spin zero (G4DecayWithSpin draws an uncorrelated direction). Results:
1973 Michels, mean cosine -0.05747 +/-0.01312, high-energy same-hemisphere
fraction0.38452. These agree statistically with the water Michel oracle.
Do not merely scale the spin vector: G4MuonDecayChannelWithSpin uses it as
a unit rotation axis and does not multiply angular asymmetry by magnitude.

Minimal fix: register G4SpinDecayPhysics after G4DecayPhysics; this installs
both pion and muon spin-aware processes and muon decay tables. Add calibrated
stopping-water depolarization. Candidate code: PhysicsList_spin.cc and
SteppingAction_spin_water.cc. ROOT positron creator becomes DecayWithSpin,
so LUCiD particle_categorization.py:44 must also accept DecayWithSpin in
DECAY_PROCESSES. Pion-to-muon creator remains Decay. See minimal_spin_fix.diff.

Minimal physical test: test_spin_physics.py compares the high-energy
hemisphere fraction to the independently integrated Michel formula for
water. Executed in39969890: baseline FAILS (0.529670 versus expected0.383488,
statistical sigma0.016119), calibrated water PASSES (0.384522, sigma0.016908).

Scope: stopped-pion daughter muons in the controlled test are below the
Cherenkov threshold; the standalone Michel marginal direction remains
isotropic. This proof establishes lost joint kinematics, not a claimed
change in total photons or 15% of all events. Its visible-light relevance
is muons born in pion decays in flight, and polarized primary muons when
their initial polarization is modeled. In the exact-source20-event pi+1GeV
fixture there are9 pi->mu->Michel chains, including3 with directly emitting
muons: birth KE96.672,1020.453,701.745MeV and direct muon Cherenkov counts
2569,191216,115585 respectively. This demonstrates a real production path;
20events do not establish a precise campaign-wide frequency or efficiency
impact. production_chain_results.json preserves the actual events.
Full mu- capture/depolarization behavior
and GENIE primary spin input are not validated by this mu+ fix.

CONFIRMED SECONDARY TRUTH DEFECT: time-inferred photon segment is sometimes
not the actual creation segment.

upstream/src/DataManager.cc:464-473 maps each photon to its immediate parent's
last segment whose start time precedes the reconstructed emission time.
In ten actual 2.2MeV gamma events, sum of per-segment Cherenkov counts equals
all1817 stored photons, but the histogram of Photon_SegmentIndex disagrees
by absoluteL1=68. This requires at least34 photons to have wrong segment
labels. Geometry independently resolves32 unambiguous wrong assignments:
photon source is >0.001mm from assigned segment but <0.001mm from another
same-parent segment (1.76% of photons). Most point to the next step.
Worst example: event0 photon56 assigned43 but emitted on42; assigned
distance0.0252856mm versus correct distance0.000006026mm. Rawfloat32 source
positions limit finer geometry checks. segment_ownership_results.json
contains explicit endpoints and photon coordinates.
The same count invariant fails for e100MeV (L1=580/110423), mu1000MeV
(2576/1061694) and pi1000MeV (11306/2380274).
Total PE and emitting-particle identity remain intact here. This is a small
sub-mm per-step truth defect, not a demonstrated large simulation-yield loss.

Minimal test: test_segment_counts.py, first gamma event only, compare
bincount(Photon_SegmentIndex) with Segment_NCherenkov. It FAILED as expected
in39969890:144photons in the first event, absolute segment mismatch8.
Geometric proof is
segment_ownership.py. Suggested minimal reliable fix is to tag each optical
secondary with its parent's current local step index during the parent's
UserSteppingAction, retain that index with the photon, and combine it with
the existing per-parent global segment offset in EndEvent. Avoid timestamp
inference or nearest-position matching for creation provenance. No fix
to this ownership code was executed during this lane.

LIMITS

These checks cannot certify all hadron/nuclear interaction models or real
detector agreement. G4HadronElasticPhysics lacks thermal water treatment;
not raised for WAND's accepted late-light/no-neutron-tagging scope. No claim
is made that GENIE nuclear remnants, source polarization, external material
near real detector boundaries, all particle species, or every energy have
been calibrated. The huge homogeneous water world plus translated/clipped
light source remains an approximation requiring near-boundary validation.
Water emission-band tails, material density and interpolation are small
modeling effects here, not established major bugs.
