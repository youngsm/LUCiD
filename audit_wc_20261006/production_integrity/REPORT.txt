Supplemental confirmed bug: pileup loses segment group_id (lower impact,
outside the supplied single-vertex SK_WAND GeV production path).

Location: lucid/sources/event_generation.py:1233,1271,1300 omits group_id from
the merged segment payload. lucid/sources/writer.py:672 falls back to arange
when it is absent. Thus two input streams with group_id[0,0] each become
persisted[0,1,2,3], rather than[0,0,1,1]. This silently changes downstream
coarse-segment grouping and any labels/counts derived from those groups.
It does not change photon tracing, sensor charge, timing or raw segment rows.
The supplied Test/01_pile_up_bombs.json defaults to HK, further limiting
relevance to the user's current SK_WAND target. Do not present this as a
large photon-yield bug.

Minimal executed reproducer: repro_pileup_group_ids.py, included by
check_production.py. The diagnostic asserts the mismatch to prove current
behavior; after fixing, make that assertion equality for a regression test.

Minimal suggested fix (not applied): preserve group_id while merging, adding
a cumulative number-of-groups offset per stream so group IDs remain unique:
